## 6. Cookies and similar technologies

### Essential cookies

The Website sets a session cookie (`_fractal_soft_session`). It is required for security (CSRF protection) and basic site features (for example success/error messages after form submission). This cookie is httpOnly, Secure, and SameSite=Lax.

### Analytics cookies (optional)

If you accept analytics cookies, we load Google Analytics (measurement ID `G-NKMWEXWYK9`). Google may set cookies such as `_ga` and `_ga_*`.

We use **Google Consent Mode v2**. Until you accept analytics:

- consent signals default to denied (`analytics_storage`, `ad_storage`, `ad_user_data`, `ad_personalization`);
- Google tags may send cookieless pings for modeling, without storing analytics cookies.

After you accept, we update Consent Mode to granted for those signals. If you choose essential cookies only, signals remain denied and analytics cookies are cleared when possible.

### Local storage

We may store in your browser (not as HTTP cookies):

- theme preference (`theme`);
- cookie consent choice (`cookie_consent`).

## 7. Retention

- Analytics data is retained according to our Google Analytics configuration and Google’s product settings.
- Cookie consent preference remains until you clear site data or change your choice in **Cookie settings**.
